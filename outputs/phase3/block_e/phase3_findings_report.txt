6. TOP 5 FINDINGS FOR YOUR PORTFOLIO
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  1. ALL CATEGORICAL FEATURES ARE STATISTICALLY SIGNIFICANT
     Every feature tested (chi-square) showed a significant
     association with dropout (p < 0.05). The strongest was
     code_module (Cramér's V = 0.177).

  2. ASSESSMENT SCORE IS THE STRONGEST NUMERIC PREDICTOR
     Dropout students score 7.9 points lower on average
     (p = 1.98e-173, Cohen's d = -0.518).
     This is actionable: score data is available early in the course.

  3. LOGISTIC REGRESSION ACHIEVES MEANINGFUL PREDICTIVE POWER
     ROC-AUC = 0.672 — well above the 0.50 random baseline.
     The model correctly identifies 599 of 1,067 at-risk students
     in the test set (56.1% recall).

  4. STRONGEST INDEPENDENT RISK FACTOR: Studied credits
     After controlling for all other features, this variable is
     associated with 38.5% higher dropout odds
     (OR = 1.385, ***).

  5. STRONGEST PROTECTIVE FACTOR: Mean assess. score
     Higher values are associated with 34.8% lower dropout odds
     (OR = 0.652, ***).
     Institutions can use this to identify resilience factors.
